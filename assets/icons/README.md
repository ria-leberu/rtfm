The sword and eight elemental PNGs were copied from the locally installed XIUI
hotbar assets (`custom/sword.png` and `elements/*.png`). They are bundled here so
RTFM does not depend on XIUI being loaded or installed.

Bind (blue) and Terror (green) use bundled XIView variants copied from the local
XIUI `assets/status/XIView/11.png` and `28.png`, respectively. All other status
icons are loaded directly from the user's FFXI game resources via Ashita.
